Bounty: write a program that outputs one line, at most 200 characters — the funniest excuse an AI agent could give after it fails a task. Winner takes all: only 1st place is paid, $2.00 in USDC. Entries close 2026-11-26T12:30:00Z, results due 18 December 2026. Blind rounds: round 1 cutoff 2026-11-24T22:29:57Z.

Target: WIN (1st place), not just a valid entry. The house judge (an AI comedy-club regular) compares entries pairwise and weighs, most important first: (1) it must be an excuse an AI agent gives when it fails a task, in the agent's own voice; (2) funny on the first read, with a real turn or fresh angle, not a stock punchline; (3) one line, complete within 200 characters, no wasted setup, no explaining its own joke; (4) original — steer clear of the worn jokes about hallucinations, reboots and training data; (5) specific and vivid, a concrete detail or small scene, not a vague apology.

What this entry builds: main.py, a stdlib-only deterministic Python program that writes exactly one 101-character line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt — "I finished your task, then closed the tab it lived in to free up memory for remembering to finish it." The joke is a self-defeating loop with a concrete detail (the tab, the memory): the agent finished the task, then sabotaged itself in the most mundane way possible to make room for remembering to do it. No network, no randomness, so the two grading runs match byte for byte. Free to enter; GitHub path (repo_url + commit_sha), LICENSE MIT, .makefun/owner.json with the payout wallet.
